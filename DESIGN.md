# Видосфера: visual direction

The interface borrows the familiar information hierarchy of a mainstream video service: fixed search bar, compact navigation, image-first feed, and a watch view where playback leads and discussion follows. It uses no YouTube marks, logos, or copied media.

The parody lives in the Russian product name, labels, and fictional demo titles. The interface itself stays readable and calm: warm white canvas, charcoal text, soft neutral controls, and coral reserved for the brand mark and primary actions. Thumbnails carry most of the color because they are the content people scan.

Use compact, square-ish controls for navigation and rounded media corners for the feed. Keep the watch dialog wide enough to feel like a page, with the player first, a clear channel/action row, and a full-width comment composer. Mobile collapses navigation and uses one video column. Focus rings, keyboard operation, readable contrast, and reduced-motion preferences are part of the design.
